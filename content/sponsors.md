## Contribute to Our Raffle and Auction

We welcome all donations to support the Timberline Race Team (TRT), a not-for-profit 501(c)(3) parent volunteer organization. Each season, during the Governor’s Cup Race at the Alpine Festival, we host a major raffle and silent auction, with proceeds directly benefiting the team. As a gear intensive sport, TRT strives to maintain and update our equipment to maximize racer experience, safety, and race process efficiency.

## Raffle and Auction Donations

Donating items to our raffle or auction is an excellent way to promote your business to hundreds of families actively involved in ski racing. Our audience spans West Virginia, Virginia, Maryland, North Carolina, and Ohio, and includes families who shop for ski equipment, apparel, services like tune-ups and waxing, and who enjoy outdoor adventures such as kayaking, biking, and dining out. Whether you donate gear, gift certificates, or unique experiences, your contribution will support the team while showcasing your business to potential customers.

We provide Facebook shoutouts, include links to your business on the online raffle page, and feature your brand on a large print-out banner displayed at the auction/raffle, offering visibility to an engaged audience of outdoor enthusiasts.

## Ways to Get Involved

There are several ways businesses and individuals can get involved and support the Timberline Race Team:

- **Donate Items:** Contribute products or services to our raffle and auction. It’s a great way to promote your business to families across the region while helping our team.
- **Reach Out to Donors:** Help us secure donations from local businesses, restaurants, or corporate sponsors.
- **Spread the Word:** Assist in raising awareness and collecting donations throughout the year.

## A Community Built on Partnership

By becoming a sponsor, you join a vital part of our community, helping to foster a strong connection between our athletes, families, and visitors. Supporting the Timberline Race Team not only champions local ski talent but also connects your business with an active, outdoor-loving audience across the region. Sponsorship provides the opportunity to build brand recognition while making a lasting impact on the future of alpine skiing.

## Why Contribute?

- **Visibility:** Donors receive Facebook shoutouts, links to their business on the online raffle page, and recognition on a large banner displayed at the event.
- **Support the Team:** All proceeds from the raffle and auction directly benefit the Timberline Race Team, helping us purchase race timing equipment and B-netting to ensure racer safety.

The Timberline Race Team (TRT) is a parent-volunteer-run, non-profit 501(c)(3) organization that has experienced tremendous growth, with the number of racers tripling in recent years. With your support, we can continue providing a thriving program for young athletes to excel in alpine ski racing.
